# Password Reset Guide

To reset your company account password:

1. Go to the self-service portal at login.company.internal/reset.
2. Enter your work email address.
3. Check your personal recovery email for a reset link (not your
   work email, since you're locked out of that).
4. Choose a new password meeting the complexity requirements: at
   least 12 characters, one number, one symbol.

If you don't receive the reset email within 10 minutes, contact the
IT helpdesk directly.
