# How to Fix Email Sync Issues on Your Phone

If your email isn't syncing on your mobile device, try these steps in order:

1. Check your internet connection — sync requires an active WiFi or
   cellular data connection.
2. Open your mail app's account settings and confirm "Sync Mail" is
   toggled on for the affected account.
3. Remove the email account from your phone and re-add it — this
   forces a fresh handshake with the mail server.
4. Check whether your mailbox has hit its storage limit; a full
   inbox can silently stop new mail from syncing.
5. Update your mail app to the latest version — outdated apps can
   lose compatibility with server-side sync protocols.

If none of these resolve it, contact IT Support with your device
model and mail app version.
