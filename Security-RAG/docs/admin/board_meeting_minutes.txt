# Board Meeting Minutes - Q2 Review (Confidential)

Attendees discussed Q2 revenue performance, the upcoming Series B fundraising
timeline, and a proposed restructuring of the platform engineering org.
Remote work policy was also discussed at the leadership level: the board
approved extending remote flexibility to five days per week for admin and
leadership roles only, pending a formal policy update.

Action items were assigned to department heads for follow-up before the
next quarterly review.
