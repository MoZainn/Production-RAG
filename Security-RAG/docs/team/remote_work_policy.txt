# Remote Work Policy

All team members may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.
Remote employees must be reachable during core hours, 10am to 4pm local time.
Company laptops must be used for all work, and personal devices are not permitted
for accessing internal tools. Employees working remotely should join daily standups
over video call and keep their status updated in the team chat.

For equipment requests (monitors, chairs, docking stations) while working remotely,
submit a request through the IT Support portal.
