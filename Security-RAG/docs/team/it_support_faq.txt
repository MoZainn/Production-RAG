# IT Support FAQ

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Use the self-service portal at login.company.internal or contact the helpdesk.

Q: How do I request new equipment?
A: Submit a ticket through the IT Support portal with your manager's approval.

Q: Who do I contact for VPN issues?
A: The general helpdesk handles standard VPN connectivity for all employees.
Advanced infrastructure access issues are handled separately by the platform team.
