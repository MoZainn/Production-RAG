# New Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the team. On your first day, IT will provision your laptop and email
account. You will be added to the team chat and calendar invites for standups.

Your manager will walk you through current projects during your first week.
Please complete the mandatory security awareness training within your first
five working days. HR will schedule a 30-minute check-in at the end of week one
to answer any questions about benefits, payroll, and time-off requests.
